# Polymarket Odds

- A topic → `search_markets` with 1-2 keywords ("fed", "bitcoin", "world series").
- "What's hot" → `trending_markets`.
- `prob` is the market-implied probability in %, not a forecast or advice. Say so briefly.
- Quote only numbers from the tool, with the market link.
